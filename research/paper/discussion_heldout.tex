The check that matters most for this data set is the one by content. Every listener in ds004015 heard the same two stories, so a network tested on new people could score well by recognising the stories rather than by reading attention. Trained without any block of the story content it was tested on, from anyone, the learned decoder lost nothing at one minute (\heldContentSixty{} against \heldStandardSixty{} with the content shared) and a few points at five minutes (\heldContentFiveMin{} against \heldStandardFiveMin{}); trained on one source study and tested on the other, it kept \heldStudySixty{} and \heldStudyFiveMin{}. These checks used one network per fold with the settings of the main search (Section~\ref{sec:decoders}), so they speak to what the decoder learns, not to its final accuracy. Set against the published numbers for electrodes around the ear (Table~\ref{tab:lit}), all of them from decoders trained on the person tested, a calibration free decoder over four electrodes per ear, each ear referenced to itself, is at least as accurate.
